# Line brief: what to look for

- Paragraphs that do not open with their point.
- Numbers without a source, date or unit.
- Contrast frames, triplets used for rhythm, abstract nouns acting as agents.
- Runs of sentences with the same length and shape.
- Hollow intensifiers and stock connectors.
- Anything the writer would not say aloud to a colleague.
