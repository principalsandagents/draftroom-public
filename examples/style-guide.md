# Style guide (example)

The long form of the voice card. Draftroom does not send this file to reviewers directly: the line brief distils it. Replace it with your own.

## Structure

- Each section answers one question, named in its heading.
- Put the finding first in every paragraph.

## Evidence

- Every number has a source, a date and a unit.
- Distinguish what a study found from what its authors recommend.

## Pre-delivery check

Read the piece aloud once. Cut any sentence you would not say to a colleague.
