# Tells catalogue (example)

Patterns that make prose read as generated. Replace or extend with your own.

- **Contrast frame.** Rejecting a position nobody held before stating the real one.
- **Triplets.** Lists of three abstract nouns used for rhythm.
- **Abstract agents.** "The data tells us", "the market decided".
- **Uniform rhythm.** Every sentence the same length and shape.
- **Hollow intensifiers.** "Truly", "deeply", "incredibly".
