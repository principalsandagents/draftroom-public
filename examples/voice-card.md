# Voice card (example)

A sample voice card. Replace it with your own: Draftroom sends this file to every reviewer, so it sets the standard each comment is judged against. Keep it short; a page is plenty.

## Write like this

- Open with the claim, the number or the decision, then explain.
- Say it directly. Concrete nouns, numbers and named examples.
- State what the evidence supports and whose judgement a claim is.
- Plain words: show, help, is, has.
- Vary sentence length. Consecutive sentences open differently.
- One set of three per section at most.

## Never

- Em dashes. Use a comma, colon, parenthesis or full stop.
- The "X, not Y" contrast in any form ("not X but Y", "it's not X, it's Y", "not just X"). Keep only the positive claim.
- Punchline endings and aphorisms. A paragraph ends on information.
- Announced reframes ("what this is really about", "comes down to").
- Trailing participles that fake analysis (", highlighting its importance").
- Announced candour ("honestly", "frankly", "to be honest").
- Stock connectors and openers ("moreover", "furthermore", "it is important to note", "in today's world").
- Filler vocabulary: delve, leverage, robust, pivotal, foster, unlock, seamless, holistic.
- Title case in headings.
