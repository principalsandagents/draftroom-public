## 0.1.0 (2026-10-03)

Initial public release.

- Markdown editor with live preview, formatting toolbar, footnotes and endnotes, and images by button, paste or drag.
- Review perspectives on demand through your own Claude and Codex logins: Argument, Evidence, Line and voice, Copy and proof. Comments anchor to the text and never write into it.
- Signals from TypeSafe's Jev, off by default and switched on per draft.
- Citations: note numbering, footnote and endnote conversion, word-for-word quote checks, AGLC 4 and Chicago 18 formatting, reference validation.
- Word import and export through pandoc.
- Lint perspective ships switched off: it needs a lint script named in your profile.
